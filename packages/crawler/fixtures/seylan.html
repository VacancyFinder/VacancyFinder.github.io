Our Success

Ours is a story of firsts 

A story that has made headlines due to our innovative revolutionary stance in leading the banking industry into new paradigms. Some of our firsts including night banking which changed the face of traditional banking and SMS banking that brought in a totally new technological dimension and a whole new vista into banking. Ours is a story that creates and enables people to grow, one that gives equal opportunity to all and empowers people from all walks of life to reach zeniths only dreamed of before.

 

"The Seylan Team is our prime competitive advantage!"

Do you like having fun, being competitive, and enjoying life in general? Do you have good interpersonal team working skills topped up with a flair for communication? Do you enjoy working in a knowledge-centric environment that is challenging but rewarding?

 

By joining the Seylan Team, you will fulfill all your aspirations - personal and professional.

Corporate Banking & Financial Markets

Salary and Other Benefits

We offer comprehensive and competitive benefits designed to meet the changing needs of all employees. With recognition and rewards linked to performance and target achievement, in sales categories especially you can reach the sky and go beyond. Basically, our policy is that you determine what you earn – and if you are a performer, there is nothing that will stop you!

Widespread multiple benefits are applicable to each grade and include housing, vehicles, computer, and educational loans at concessionary rates with welfare and medical insurance also added onto an ever-lengthening list of benefits.



Work-Life Balance

One of our most significant rewards is that we pay you an incentive to go on vacation because we believe that a healthy work-life balance ensures better productivity and total contentment in an individual.



Learning and Development:

We recognize and acknowledge the capabilities and competencies of people and bridge knowledge gaps through extensive training and development opportunities imparted through multi-faceted learning and teaching methodologies.
