The employees at Abans Finance PLC are empowered to build long-term relationships with our Customers.

Senior Executive/Executive/Junior Executive/Trainee Executive – Marketing

Key Responsibilities
• To achieve set targets on Micro Leasing (registered two/ three-wheeler) and Auto Finance products within the credit
guidelines.
• Maintain desired collection ratio.
• Maintain operational controls and customer service excellence

Requirements:

  • In terms of Senior Executive and Executive positions mentioned above, minimum 2 years of experience in the field of marketing, credit evaluation and portfolio management of micro leasing (two-wheeler & three-wheeler) and / or auto finance products in a non-bank finance company registered with Central Bank of Sri Lanka and good knowledge of analyzing the creditworthiness of clients are required.
  • School leavers with O/L qualification and extracurricular activities are also encouraged to apply for Trainee / Junior Executive positions.
  • Should possess a valid riding/ driving license.
  • Candidates with full/part professional/academic qualifications in a related area though not essential but will be an advantage.
  • Applications from candidates residing in Muttur and Kinniya areas are encouraged.

Gold Loan In Charge & Assessor

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Advancing within the approved limits set out in the standardized procedure guidelines.
  • Checking gold articles, doing valuation and assessment of articles, and negotiating with customers
    regarding advances under the given guidelines.
  • Ensure strict adherence to all operational processes and guidelines pertaining to Gold Financing.
  • Strategically plan and oversee the smooth execution of the Gold Loan business, while developing
    promotional activities to foster business growth.
  • Continuously monitor the performance of the Gold Loan portfolio and implement necessary actions to
    drive advances and enhance portfolio growth.
  • Stay informed on and comply with all relevant regulatory requirements.

Requirements:

  • GCE (A/L) qualification
  • A minimum of 2-3 years of experience in pawning or gold loan operations within a recognized financial institution.
  • Proficiency in identifying counterfeit or substandard jewelry.
  • Expertise in evaluating the gold content of articles.
  • Excellent interpersonal and customer relationship management skills
  • Strong communication and negotiation abilities.
  • Age between 25-35 years.
