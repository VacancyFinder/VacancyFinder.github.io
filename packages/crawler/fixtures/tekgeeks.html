Build Your Career With Us

We're always looking for talented, driven individuals who want to do the best work of their careers. If you thrive in a collaborative, high-performance environment, we want to hear from you. Send your CV to hrm@tekgeeks.net.

Full-Stack Developer

We’re seeking a Full-Stack Developer with 3 to 5 years of industry experience to join our team and drive the development of our web applications, portals, and backend systems.

Location: Nawala, Sri Lanka

Employment Type: Full-time

Experience: Minimum 3 years of industry experience in Software Development

System Administrator

We’re seeking a System Administrator with a minimum of 3 years of industry experience to join our team and support the infrastructure behind our websites, web applications, portals, workflow automation systems, and cloud environments.

Location: Rajagiriya, Sri Lanka

Employment Type: Full-time

Experience: Minimum 3 years of industry experience in System Administration

Business Development Executive

We're looking for a Business Development Executive to join our team and drive growth, strengthen client relationships, and support the operational functions that underpin our project delivery.

Location: Nawala, Sri Lanka

Employment Type: Full-time

Experience: Minimum 2 years of professional experience in a relevant field


About the Role

A client-facing, commercially minded role for someone who enjoys building relationships, identifying opportunities, and keeping a growing business running smoothly behind the scenes. You'll work directly with leadership to expand our client base, manage key accounts, and handle the operational coordination that supports our development teams. If you're equally comfortable pitching to a prospective client and coordinating a project delivery, you'll fit right in.

Life at TekGeeks

Life at TekGeeks is driven by a culture that values initiative, creativity, and bold thinking. Every team member is empowered to be a leader without a title, encouraged to take ownership, challenge norms, and contribute ideas that make a real impact. This environment fosters accountability, collaboration, and continuous growth both individually and as a company.

Our teams are passionate and united by a shared vision of innovation and excellence. Through strong collaboration and knowledge sharing, we stay aligned with emerging technologies to deliver outstanding results. At the same time, we remain committed to social responsibility, actively giving back to the community and creating a positive impact beyond the workplace.
