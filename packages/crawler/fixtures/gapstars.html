Our values

Values that make us

The Gapstars Community
This isn’t about creating another tech workplace. To us, it’s about building genuine connections where your contribution shapes our collective success.
Growth that matters
Your development matters, both professionally and personally. We champion ambitious goals and provide the support to achieve them, but we believe in mutual commitment. It’s about growing together.
Meaningful impact
Technology is our tool, not our end goal. Beyond delivering exceptional solutions, we’re actively involved in social responsibility programmes, including our structured initiative supporting education from early years through to professional development.
Our selection journey

Our Process

  • Your application

Connect with us through our Careers section. Our Talent specialists will have an honest conversation about your expertise and aspirations.

  • Technical Assessment

Demonstrate your capabilities through our Engineering team’s assessment. Success here leads to a partner-specific technical evaluation.

  • Final Interviews
Meet our specialists and partners for comprehensive technical and personal discussions. This is where we ensure the right match — for you and our partners.

“We focus on finding the perfect alignment between talent and opportunity. Our process is thorough because we're building lasting relationships, not just filling positions.”

Natasha Gunatilleke

Manager: Portugal Recruitment

  • Final Interviews

You’ve reached the last stage! This involves Technical and HR interviews with specialists and partners, so be ready to show off your technical prowess and confidence. If you impress, you’ll officially become part of the Gapstars Community and integrated within our partner teams.

Transform ambition into achievement with our tailored learning solutions

The Gapstars Academy forms the cornerstone of our commitment to continuous learning. As part of our community, you’ll have access to curated professional development opportunities that aim to go beyond traditional training. Our Academy team provides ongoing support to help you thrive throughout your journey with Gapstars.

Cultural Awareness Training

Our approach to cross-cultural collaboration is based on Erin Meyer’s ‘The Culture Map’ framework. Together, we’ll explore the eight key dimensions of international business, from communication styles to decision-making approaches.

Learning Hub

Our learning hub houses over 200 carefully selected courses and resources. Beyond traditional e-learning, the Space Portal offers: live training sessions with industry experts, technical deep-dives, self-paced professional development tracks and practical workshops.

Soft Skills Workshops

Our development programme supports your growth as a complete professional through. Ranging from focused workshops on leadership and communication to practical sessions on project and time management. Choose between instructor-led sessions or self-paced learning through our Space Portal.

Technical Workshops

Technical growth starts from day one at Gapstars. Each programme is designed to build both theoretical understanding and practical capability. Our practical workshops include: agile methodology using hands-on LEGO exercises, pathways to Scrum Master certification, product thinking and development and emerging technology deep-dives.
