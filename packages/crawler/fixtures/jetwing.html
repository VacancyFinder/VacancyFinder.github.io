Careers

Join us for a rewarding career in Sri Lanka’s flourishing hospitality industry. Growing faster than ever before, our island’s tourism sector is one of the most exciting and opportunistic industries to be a part of. As you work with our natural wonders, meet people from around the world, and be forever inspired by our legendary stories, Sri Lanka is undoubtedly one of the world’s finest working environments for a career in tourism and hospitality.

At Jetwing Hotels, we are proud to be the home of Sri Lankan hospitality – an equal opportunity employer welcoming individuals of all backgrounds to our family. As we believe in the importance of supporting every member, our Career Progress and Development Programmes have also been designed to serve our employees with superior direction and training. In turn, we hope to assist you in achieving your professional goals to the best of your abilities, in a friendly environment built on mutual respect.

Regardless of the department you choose to build your career in, our family is one that is tight-knit, progressive and globally minded. A wealth of opportunities await your passions – from designing unforgettable culinary experiences in our Food and Beverage Department, building our enchanting brand in our Sales and Marketing Department, or even supporting the internal systems at each of our hotels and villas from our Information Systems and Technology Department, there will always be something for you at Jetwing Hotels.

Browse through our list of current vacancies below, divided into each of our departments should you possess a specific skillset that would be a welcome addition to any Jetwing property across Sri Lanka. In the event that you do not come across an opportunity that appeals to you, you are more than welcome to send us a general application at careers@jetwinghotels.com. At the home of Sri Lankan hospitality, our doors are always open.

Food & Beverages Department

The Food and Beverages Department which is responsible for maintaining the high quality of food and service to the hotel’s guests, provides abundant opportunities and career development skills for its associates. At Jetwing properties the Food and Beverage teams seek to create a restaurant, bar or banquet experience that exceeds guest expectations by providing the exceptional quality and service which contribute to the overall success and reputation of our individual hotels.

Front Office Department

Considered as the nerve centre of a hotel; the function of a Front Office Department is to act as the public face of the hotel by greeting and checking in guests and ensuring their well being during their stay. Guests have the most interaction with the associates in the Front Office and a warm welcome and helpful attitude goes a long way in creating a lasting memory in the mind of a guest.

Kitchen Department

The Kitchen Department is the heart of the hotel and the inspiration centre for the preparation of culinary delights. Every hotel menu is packed with flair and imagination and a touch of individuality, ensuring that every craving of a guest is met with mouthwatering food. Our associates in the kitchen are passionate about the food they create and work hard to meet the demands of the food and beverage operations of the hotel

Spa / Fitness / Recreation

A variety of services and facilities to enhance the well being and health of guests are offered by the Spa/Fitness and Recreation Department. The Spa provides a number of rejuvenating body and facial treatments, while tennis and swimming are part of the activities offered at the fitness centre. The team of experts in this field are passionately committed to creating and maintaining an environment that is geared to improve the fitness and health of guests.

Housekeeping Department

The housekeeping department plays a crucial role in ensuring exceptional guest experience. The impact of its function on the success of a hotel cannot be underestimated. The associates in the housekeeping department strive to provide guests with clean and comfortable accommodation while away from home. From fresh bed linen to spotless bathrooms and immaculate lobbies, our associates are committed to uplifting the Jetwing standards of quality and cleanliness at all times.

Engineering & Maintenance

The Engineering and Maintenance department plays an important role in maintaining a safe and fully functioning hotel building and grounds. Ensuring that the hotel remains in peak operating condition, the department is responsible for the maintenance and repair of the physical building as well as its electrical and mechanical equipment. In addition to daily maintenance and full scale renovation projects, this department is also responsible for the conservation of waste and energy.

Sales & Marketing

The Sales and Marketing department is responsible to promote, sell and book business for the hotel, and provide an assessment of where the hotel is positioned in the marketplace. With a goal to ensure maximum occupancy in the hotel at all times, the Sales and Marketing team also co-ordinates advertising promotions and public relations activities which are aimed at enhancing the image of the hotel in the market.

Finance Department

The Finance Department is in charge of all the financial activities of the hotel. This department is responsible to record and analyze the expenses of the hotel’s operations, create an accurate account of the assets, liabilities and financial transactions, and prepare budgets and allocate revenue for the various departments of the hotel. The Finance department maintains all account related books in accordance with government rules and regulations and oversees the Payroll and purchasing departments.

Human Resource Department/Training & Development

We in the Jetwing family know that our associates are the organization’s key assets. In the Human Resources Department we focus on empowering our associates while creating a great work atmosphere where each and every team member’s contribution is valid. This department handles the recruitment and selection of employees, training, administration and overall employee relations while also providing an array of assistance to associates in a supportive and friendly manner.

Information Systems & Technology

Information resources play an important role in successful hotel operations. At Jetwing properties the Information Systems and Technology departments are service and support oriented and the associates work tirelessly to ensure that the computer systems in the hotel function properly at all times. This department is responsible to provide support for the internal hotel systems as well as making sure that hotel guests are able to stay connected at all times.

Carrying out exciting excursions to bring our guests face to face with the natural world; educating guests, staff, and the local communities about our ecosystems and animals; and backing Jetwing Hotels’ extensive efforts towards sustainability are all in a day’s work for our expert team of passionate wildlife enthusiasts.
