Choreo Labs Careers

Where passion meets purpose

Our culture is powered by one simple value: treat people the way you want to be treated. This means we treat everyone in a fair, open, honest and respectful manner from the moment you apply for a role at Choreo Labs.

View Open Positions→

Our maxims

We have no office rules and our culture is our way of life. Our four maxims guide us in how we treat each other across our global team.

Trust and openness

We enable fluidity of information, give and receive feedback, and refrain from micromanaging teams.

Courageous honesty

We don’t have hidden agendas, we’re honest with ourselves and others, and discuss successes and failures openly.

Purpose and passion

We nurture a culture of meritocracy where performance and meaningful contribution are valued and rewarded.

One team

We don’t have space for ‘jerks’ - we respect each other, even if we disagree, and focus on solutions instead of pointing fingers.

Interview process
what to expect

The interview process at Choreo Labs involves multiple conversations about your qualifications, skills, and what motivates you. It’s a chance for us to ensure you’re a strong match for our company culture, and just as importantly, it allows you to determine if Choreo Labs aligns with your own career aspirations.

  1. Step 1

    Each candidate goes through three independent, first-level interviews.

    Reasons for votes are provided to ensure transparency.

  2. Step 2

    Candidates with at least two +1 votes move to this level where they meet our senior functional leads and directors.

    Meet the HR team to discuss employee benefits and work culture.

  3. Step 3

    Meet the CEO.

    This conversation will focus on culture and your role in upkeeping what’s important to us.
