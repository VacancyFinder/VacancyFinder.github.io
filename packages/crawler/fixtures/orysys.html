Tech Lead position

CBC Tech Solutions Limited, a fully owned subsidiary IT Company of Commercial Bank of Ceylon PLC has
the following opportunity for dynamic, energetic young candidates.

We are searching of an energetic individual with a strong engineering background to develop scalable
consumer-facing mobile applications and integrating them with web services. The person should take the
ownership of the entire Software Development lifecycle of mobile applications and collaborate with cross functional teams to define and design new features. Furthermore, ensure the performance, quality, and
responsiveness of the applications, constantly learning about new technologies to improve development
efficiency

 

SENIOR SOFTWARE ENGINEER – MOBILE (ANDROID/iOS)

Job Description

  • Design, Plan and implement reliable and high-performance applications.
  • Ability to handle multiple projects in parallel.
  • Set a benchmark in productivity, quality, and delivery of a quality software meeting all criteria.
  • Research and deploy new and emerging technology and is responsible for maintaining a high level
    of expertise in all areas of technology thus used.
  • Ability to make use of this knowledge to constantly build the skills of other team members.
  • Keep the Management team up to date on development and project progress.

 

Applicant’s Profile

  • Bachelor’s Degree in Computer, Information Systems, Information Technology, Software
    Engineering, or equivalent.
  • A minimum of 4 years of work experience in industry level.
  • High Proficiency and hands-on experience in iOS/ Android.
  • Willingness to learn different programming paradigms, languages and technologies as of the
    requirement.
  • Knowledge of latest frameworks like Rx, Dagger, Realm, Hands-on experience of Backend
  • development and Test Automation will be added value.
  • Hands-on experience of Kotlin, Flutter and React Native will added advantage.
